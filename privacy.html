Palmpin Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. This policy covers the iOS app Palmpin: Map for GTA 6 ("Palmpin").

Short version

Palmpin has no user accounts, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your map progress, favorites, notes, personal markers, and saved filters stay on your device (and optionally in your private iCloud account).

Data stored on your device

  • Checklist progress, playthrough profiles, favorites, personal notes, saved filter sets, and preferences.
  • Personal markers and any in-game screenshots you choose to attach from your Photo Library. Attached photos are copied into the app's local storage on your device.

Deleting the app removes local data. You can also clear progress or personal markers at any time in Settings.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud is signed in on your Apple devices, Palmpin uses Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore to sync your checklist progress across your own devices. This data lives in your private Apple iCloud account and is never accessible to us.

Photos & voluntary community submissions

Palmpin requests Photo Library access only when you explicitly choose a screenshot for a personal marker or when you tap Add photo to submit a spot screenshot. If you choose to send a report, location suggestion, or screenshot, Palmpin opens your Mail app so you can review and send the email yourself. We do not upload your photos automatically in the background.

Purchases

In-app purchases (including the 3-day free trial and lifetime Pro unlock) are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat solely to verify your purchase entitlements. RevenueCat receives your App Store receipt and an anonymous app-generated identifier — never your notes, markers, or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Network requests

Core map tiles (zoom levels 3–5), pin data, and bundled photos work offline. When you are online and zoom to maximum detail (zoom level 6), Palmpin fetches tile images from map.gtadb.org. Palmpin may also check our static content.json update feed on GitHub Pages for updated location data.

Children

Palmpin is a companion tool for a mature-rated game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
