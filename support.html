Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3-Day Free Trial work?

The 3-day trial is a free ($0) one-time starter pass — it is not a subscription and will never auto-renew or charge you automatically. When the 3 days end, you can choose whether to unlock Lifetime Pro with a single one-time purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open Palmpin → tap Restore on the unlock screen (or go to Settings → Restore Purchases) while signed into the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

How do I back up or transfer my progress?

Checklist progress syncs automatically across your Apple devices via iCloud. You can also export and import a full JSON backup anytime in Settings → Backup.

How do I submit a spot screenshot or fix a pin?

Tap any pin on the map and press Add photo to email your own in-game screenshot (with optional credit name), or tap the flag icon to report a wrong spot or description.
